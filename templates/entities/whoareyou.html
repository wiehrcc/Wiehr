Wiehr — Electronic Composer & Multidisciplinary Artist

Yauheni 'Wiehr' Kandratovich

Wiehr is a pseudonymous Belarus-born electronic composer and multidisciplinary artist creating cinematic, ambient, and experimental music through a synthetic orchestra approach.

Built on direct emotion, words, and sound, his work remains both fragile and powerful. His work combines synthetic organ, minimal rock drums, string ensembles, and cyberpunk bass — moving across genres to form a distinct internal world.

Released through Label Nisha with self-engineered cover art shaped by raw color and emotion. Featured in campaigns across Belarus, Ukraine, Russia, and the EU — built entirely by a single composer and engineer, creating a direct, personal signature.
